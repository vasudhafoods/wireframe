Vasudha Foods: Sattvic Food, from the House of Hare Krishna

Vasudha Foods is India's trusted name in Sattvic, no-onion-no-garlic food — bringing the same recipes served in ISKCON temple kitchens into everyday homes. From ready-to-eat meals like Dal Khichadi and Rajma Chawal to millet noodles, cookies and festive snacks, every product is prepared with pure, mindful ingredients and no preservatives or maida. Whether you're observing Upvas, cooking for a joint family, or simply choosing a cleaner way to eat, Vasudha makes wholesome, devotional food easy to find and quick to prepare. Our combo packs, gifting boxes and pan-India delivery bring Sattvic nourishment to thousands of families, with the same care and consistency as a temple kitchen.
